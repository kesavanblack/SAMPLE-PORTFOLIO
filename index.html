About Me

A closer look at how I work with data.

I'm a Data Analyst based in Coimbatore with two years of hands-on experience turning raw, messy data into insights that businesses can act on. My day-to-day work spans SQL, Power BI, Python, and Excel — from cleaning and transforming datasets to building ETL pipelines that feed reliable, decision-ready dashboards.
